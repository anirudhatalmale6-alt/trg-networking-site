Common questions

What business leaders ask us.

Can TRG support organizations outside Maryland?
Yes. TRG is headquartered in Columbia, Maryland and provides remote managed services and technology support to organizations nationwide.
Do you replace an internal IT person?
TRG can serve as a complete outsourced IT team or work alongside internal technology staff, depending on your needs.
Is managed IT billed monthly?
Managed services are generally structured around a predictable monthly agreement. The exact scope depends on your users, locations, systems and support requirements.
